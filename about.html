About NirmanTools BD

NirmanTools BD is a lightweight Bangladesh-focused construction calculation project. Our goal is to make common construction estimates easier to understand and calculate.

Important disclaimer

Calculator outputs are estimates and should not replace an engineer's design, project-specific BOQ, structural calculation or professional quotation. Material prices are illustrative and should be updated according to current local market rates.
